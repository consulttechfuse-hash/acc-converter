What is Money?

Money is anything widely accepted as payment. Today, most money is just numbers in a computer — not gold, not paper.

🏛️

Central Bank Money

Cash notes and coins you can hold. Also the digital reserves banks keep at the central bank. The most trusted type — it's the government's money.

Most trusted
💳

Bank Account Money

The balance in your bank app. Created when banks make loans — when a bank lends you R100,000, it types that number into your account.

Most common
📜

Fiat Money

Money backed by law and trust — not gold. Its value comes from government declaring it legal tender and central banks managing the economy.

Government-backed

The Two-Tier Banking System

Banks don't work in isolation. There's a hierarchy — central bank at the top, commercial banks in the middle, and you at the bottom.

🏛️

Central Bank

The bank's bank. Issues currency, controls interest rates, acts as a safety net. Banks keep reserves here.

🏦

Commercial Banks

Standard Bank, FNB, Absa. Take deposits, lend, licensed by the central bank.

🏢

Government

Collects taxes, pays for services, sets the rules. Central bank implements policy.

👤

You

You bank with commercial banks, not the central bank directly. Payments move through the layers above before settling.

How a Payment Moves

When you tap your card or send an EFT, a lot happens invisibly in milliseconds.

📱
You Pay
Tap card, app, or checkout
🔍
Processor
Checks fraud, routes transaction
🏦
Your Bank
Checks funds, approves or declines
⚖️
Clearing
Banks agree on who owes what
✅
Settlement
Real money moves via central bank reserves
💡 Key insight: Money doesn't instantly leave your bank. First authorisation, then clearing, then settlement — seconds or days depending on the system.

How Visa & Mastercard Work

They don't hold your money or issue your card. They're the network connecting four players every time you swipe.

👤

You

Tap card or phone at the till.

🏪

Merchant

The shop. Their card machine comes from their bank.

🌐

Network

Visa/Mastercard — sets rules, routes, coordinates. Doesn't hold money.

🏦

Issuer

Your bank. Approves or declines each transaction.

Fees on every transaction

💸 Interchangepaid to your bank
🌐 Networkpaid to Visa/MC
🏪 Acquirerpaid to merchant's bank

Apps That Aren't Banks

Not every payment app is a bank — even if it looks like one.

💶

E-Money Institutions

Wise, some Revolut accounts. Can hold money and make payments. Funds safeguarded but not insured like bank deposits. Can't lend.

Safeguarded, not insured
🔀

Payment Processors

Stripe, Adyen, PayFast. Process card payments for businesses. They don't hold your personal funds.

Plumbing only
📲

Mobile Money

M-Pesa, MTN MoMo. Store money on your phone SIM — no bank account needed. Funds pooled in a trust account.

SIM-based
🌍

Digital Banks

Capitec, TymeBank, Discovery Bank. These ARE banks — fully licensed, deposit-insured, regulated by SARB.

Full bank licence
⚠️ Rule of thumb: Money in a licensed bank is covered by deposit insurance. Money in an app that isn't a bank may be safeguarded but not insured.

How Central Banks Create Money

A central bank uses specific tools to inject or withdraw money from the banking system.

📋

Open Market Operations

CB buys government bonds from banks. Bank reserves credited — money created from nothing. Selling reverses it.

Main tool
🔁

Repo Rate

Banks borrow reserves overnight. Collateral is government bonds. Sets the floor for all rates.

Rate anchor
💧

Reserve Requirements

Lowering the minimum reserve % lets banks lend more — expanding money.

Legacy tool
📡

Standing Facilities

Always-on windows for banks to deposit surplus or borrow emergency funds.

Always-on
💡 Key insight: Central banks create reserves (M0), not the broader money supply. Commercial banks multiply it through lending.

The Money Supply: M0 to M3

Economists measure money using "M" levels. Each is broader — it includes the one before plus less-liquid forms.

M0

Cash + Central Bank Reserves

Notes, coins, plus electronic reserves banks hold at the CB. "Base money."

NARROW
M1

M0 + Demand Deposits

Everyday bank accounts you can access instantly — by card, EFT, or ATM.

NARROW
M2

M1 + Savings + Short-term

Savings accounts, money-market funds, short-term fixed deposits. SARB mainly watches this.

BROAD
M3

M2 + Large Deposits

Longer-term deposits and large instruments. Key indicator of credit in the economy.

WIDEST
💡 "Printing money" or QE mainly expands M0. Banks then multiply it through lending — expanding M1, M2, M3.

NOSTRO & VOSTRO

Banks don't have branches everywhere. They hold accounts at foreign banks — correspondent banking.

🇿🇦

Standard Bank (SA)

Wants to send USD to a US client. Needs US banking access but has no US branch.

🇺🇸

Citibank (USA)

Acts as Standard Bank's USD correspondent. Holds USD on their behalf.

"Our account with you"
Std Bank's USD at Citi
VOSTRO
"Your account with us"
Same account, Citi's view
🔑 NOSTRO = "ours" (abroad). VOSTRO = "yours" (held with us). Same account — two perspectives.

Banking Instruments

Large transactions — especially international ones — require formal banking instruments.

🛡️

Bank Guarantee / SBLC

Bank promises to pay a third party if their client fails. Backed by bank's creditworthiness.

BG / SBLC
📄

Letter of Credit

Bank commits to pay the seller once shipping conditions are met. Backbone of trade finance.

LC / L/C
📡

SWIFT Messages

MT799 is a free-format confirmation. MT760 issues a guarantee. Widely misunderstood.

MT799 / MT760
✍️

POA / RWA

POA grants legal authority. RWA confirms verified available funds before a big deal.

POA / RWA
