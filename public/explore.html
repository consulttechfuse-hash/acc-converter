What is Money?

Money is anything widely accepted as payment. Today, most money is just numbers in a computer — not gold, not paper.

🏛️

Central Bank Money

Cash notes and coins you can hold. Also the digital reserves banks keep at the central bank. The most trusted type.

💳

Bank Account Money

The balance in your bank app. Created when banks make loans — when a bank lends you R100,000, it types that number into your account.

📜

Fiat Money

Money backed by law and trust — not gold. Its value comes from government declaring it legal tender.

Explore · 02

How a Payment Moves

When you tap your card or send an EFT, a lot happens invisibly in milliseconds.

📱

You Pay

Tap card, app, or checkout.

🔍

Processor

Checks fraud, routes transaction.

🏦

Your Bank

Checks funds, approves or declines.

⚖️

Clearing

Banks agree on who owes what.

✅

Settlement

Real money moves via central bank reserves.

Explore · 03

How Visa & Mastercard Work

They don't hold your money or issue your card. They're the network connecting four players every time you swipe.

👤

You

Tap card or phone at the till.

🏪

Merchant

The shop. Their card machine comes from their bank.

🌐

Network

Visa/Mastercard — sets rules, routes, coordinates.

🏦

Issuer

Your bank. Approves or declines each transaction.

Explore · 04

Apps That Aren't Banks

Not every payment app is a bank — even if it looks like one.

💶

E-Money Institutions

Wise, Revolut. Can hold money and make payments. Funds safeguarded but not insured.

🔀

Payment Processors

Stripe, PayFast. Process card payments for businesses. They don't hold your funds.

📲

Mobile Money

M-Pesa, MTN MoMo. Store money on your phone SIM — no bank needed.

🌍

Digital Banks

Capitec, TymeBank. These ARE banks — fully licensed and regulated.

Explore · 05

Fiat, Crypto, CBDC & Stablecoins

Not all "digital money" is the same. Here's how the four main types compare.

💵

Fiat

Rand, Dollar, Euro. Created by banks & CBs. Backed by law.

🏛️

CBDC

Digital cash from a central bank. Like an e-rand. Backed 100%.

₿

Crypto

Bitcoin, Ethereum. No central authority. Volatile.

🔒

Stablecoin

USDT, USDC. Crypto pegged to fiat. Fast global transfers.

Global & Regional Rails

Different systems move money across borders. Here are the key ones.

🌐

SWIFT

🌍

🤝

BRICS Pay

Connects BRICS payment systems. Cross-border QR.

🇮🇳

UPI

India's real-time payment system.

🏦

BIS

💰

IMF

Monitors the global economy and lends emergency funds.

🌱

World Bank

🚨

FATF

How Central Banks Create Money

A central bank uses specific tools to inject or withdraw money from the banking system.

📋

Open Market Operations

CB buys government bonds from banks. Bank reserves credited.

🔁

Repo Rate

Banks borrow reserves overnight. Sets the floor for all rates.

💧

Reserve Requirements

Lowering the minimum reserve % lets banks lend more.

Explore · 09

The Money Supply: M0 to M3

Economists measure money using "M" levels. Each is broader than the last.

M0

Cash + Central Bank Reserves. "Base money."

M1

M0 + Demand Deposits. Everyday bank accounts.

M2

M1 + Savings + Short-term. Broad money.

M3

M2 + Large Deposits. Widest measure.

KYC, AML & Compliance

Before any bank or platform lets you move money, they must verify who you are.

🪪

KYC

Know Your Customer. ID verification, Due Diligence, Enhanced Due Diligence.

🚨

AML

Anti-Money Laundering. Transaction monitoring and reporting.

Banking Instruments

Large transactions require formal banking instruments.

🛡️

Bank Guarantee / SBLC

Bank promises to pay if their client fails.

📄

Letter of Credit

Bank commits to pay once shipping conditions are met.

📡

SWIFT MT799/MT760

Free-format confirmation and guarantee issuance.
